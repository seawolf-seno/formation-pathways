# Capture on your phone: staff guide

Formation Pathways lets you record, in a few seconds, a moment you saw in a
student: kindness, courage, a question that mattered. Those moments build that
student's Pathway. Nothing you capture is shown to a student or family, and any
AI-written summary is a **Draft** until a named adult signs it.

## 1. Sign in (once)

1. On your phone, open **https://formation-pathways.vercel.app/capture**.
2. Sign in with your `@pacificachristian.org` account: **Continue with
   Google** if you see it, otherwise enter your email and type the 6-digit
   code you're sent.
3. First time only: you'll see "waiting for approval". seno approves you, and
   then you're in.

## 2. Put it on your home screen

- **iPhone (Safari):** Share → *Add to Home Screen*.
- **Android (Chrome):** ⋮ menu → *Install app*.

The **Capture** icon opens straight to capture and stays signed in. If the
home-screen app asks you to sign in again, use the 6-digit code; an email link
opens in a different browser.

## 3. Capture a moment

1. Tap the mic and say the student's name and what you saw: *"Maya stayed
   after class to help a new student find the library."*
2. Tap **Done**, check the name it matched (tap *Wrong student* if needed),
   then **Save**.

No mic? Type into the box, or use your keyboard's own dictation button.

## What to capture, and what not to

- **Yes:** specific, observed moments of growth, character, faith, curiosity
  and care, including a student working through a hard moment.
- **No:** discipline, grades, health or family circumstances, or anything you
  wouldn't be comfortable with the family reading. Discipline goes through the
  usual channels.
- Write about students the way you'd talk about them to someone who loves them.

## Good to know

- You'll only see students whose families have given consent for this beta.
- Your name is on every moment you capture, and you can only capture as yourself.
- Voice dictation is transcribed by Apple (Safari) or Google (Chrome). If
  that's a concern, type instead.
- Lost your phone? Tell seno right away so he can remove your access.

Questions or something not working: email seno@pacificachristian.org with what
you tapped and what you saw.
