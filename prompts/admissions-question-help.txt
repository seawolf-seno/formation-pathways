What were your hands or your mind busy with? | What did you forget to do because of it? | Is it something you would do again tomorrow, unasked?
Think of one specific moment, not a general trait. | What did they do that surprised you? | What would you copy from them, and what would you leave?
You don't have to pick your biggest challenge. | What did you do first? | What would you tell a friend facing the same thing?
What would you happily practice on a bad day? | Who could you learn it from? | What would you make, or do, once you were good?
Think about who you'd like to be when you walk across the stage, not what you'd have. | What would a friend say about you then? | What's one small step toward it this year?
