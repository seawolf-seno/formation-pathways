LIVE SIGNATURE PROGRAMS (real, currently offered)

- STEM Scholars: for students concentrating in science/tech/engineering/math.
  Clubs: Drone Club (competes in the Aerial Drone Competition), Makers Space
  (student-led build/innovation workshop), Women in STEM (service-oriented),
  Chem Club. A "STEM Scholar with Distinction" award exists via 8+ STEM courses
  (3+ AP), a 3.7 weighted GPA, and 100 hours of external STEM pursuits presented
  to the community.
- The Minchin Center for Economics & Innovation: AP Economics and Economics &
  Entrepreneurship (Shark-Tank-style pitches); The Wolf Shack, a real student-run
  campus store (inventory, pricing, sales, profit); Young Entrepreneurs
  Association; a DECA chapter; This Little Light Africa Club (social
  entrepreneurship supporting farming/solar/education in West Africa); the
  Insight Speaker Series bringing in outside professionals to review pitches and
  guest-teach.
- The Center for Philosophy & Theology (director: Stephen Roberson): Oxford-style
  small-group seminars on first principles — courses include Old Testament, New
  Testament, Moral Philosophy, Ancient Philosophy, Modern Philosophy, Political
  Philosophy, Patristic Theology. Values "Intellectual Hospitality" (understand
  differing views rather than attack them) and "Leisure in Learning" (deep
  reflection over constant output).
- PACARTS (the arts program): theater, visual arts, film/photography, music, and
  dance, taught by working artist-instructors; over half the student body
  participates. The i.Sight Festival is an end-of-year, three-night showcase
  across every discipline; there's also an annual Student Film Festival
  screening at a local theater.
- Athletics: philosophy of "process over product" — player development and
  character alongside winning, measured by CIF championships. The "Wolfpack"
  student section supports home games. Athletes can specialize or play multiple
  sports across their four years.
- Spiritual Life: weekly all-school chapel (prayer, singing, teaching). An
  annual All-School Retreat (small-group discussions on real-life and faith
  questions, run in partnership with Young Life since 2007, held in Arizona)
  is the year's deepest shared formation experience. Open, honest conversation
  about faith is welcomed regardless of a student's background.

PROPOSED / IN DEVELOPMENT (internal roadmap — describe as upcoming, not live yet)

- "Lost Canyon": the school's proposed re-framing of the All-School Retreat as a
  fully phone-free keystone harvest — describe as "the retreat Pacifica is
  building toward," not as an already-enforced no-phones policy.
- Pathway Expeditions: proposed small-group (2 adults, 6 students) 6–7 day
  academic road trips (e.g. Mammoth, the Sierra) blending Socratic seminar with
  outdoor skill-building. Coming, not yet running.
- The Forge / Advanced AI Agents class: a proposed school-hosted pipeline for
  students to ship their own software from laptop to a real dev server to
  production. Coming, not yet running.

GOVERNING PRINCIPLE
AI works for the adults, behind the scenes. A Pathway is a forward-looking
compass, never a backward-looking record or a ranking. Struggle is framed as
growth-in-progress. Never diagnose, label, or score a student's character.
Never invent a personal fact, habit, or teaching-style claim about a real,
named faculty member — only their real department and what that department
or program actually offers.
