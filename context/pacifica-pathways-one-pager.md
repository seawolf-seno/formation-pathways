# Formation Pathways — Executive Summary

**Pacifica Christian High School**

> **Every school can tell you what your child knows. Pacifica will be the first school that can show you who your child is becoming — and where that person will matter most.**

---

## The Idea

Pacifica's advantage is people: adults who love God, love teens, love their content, teach expertly, and love the mission. They transform teenagers into who God created them to be — but that transformation is invisible to prospective families, hard to name for current families, and unguided by data. **Formation Pathways** fixes this with one governing principle: **AI works for the adults, behind the scenes; students stay present, in person, with people.** Screen time goes down, not up.

## How It Works

Teachers capture 20–60 second observations of formation moments (a voice memo after class). AI transcribes, tags them against a faculty-authored Formation Framework rooted in grace and truth, and synthesizes 100+ witnessed moments per student over four years into a living, forward-looking **Pathway** — a compass, never a dossier: student-visible, student-co-authored, and never used to rank, gatekeep, or punish. A built-in radar flags any student going unnoticed, making Pacifica structurally a school where no kid is invisible.

## What It Produces

**For current families** — Semester Pathway Updates (advisor-signed narratives of who your child is becoming), and a senior capstone where each student authors their own four-year transformation story.

**For students' futures** — The Pathways Engine matches each student's evidence of demonstrated agency to the widest map of launch routes: selective colleges *and* the Thiel Fellowship, Y Combinator, Emergent Ventures, gap-year fellowships, trade apprenticeships, service academies, conservatories, research tracks, and ministry. College counseling becomes calling guidance.

**For prospective families** — Admissions shifts from claiming transformation to displaying it: real permissioned stories and a graduate outcomes map no other school can print.

## The Signature Experiences

**Lost Canyon (keystone).** The all-school, phone-free Arizona retreat becomes the Pathway's richest annual harvest — no technology crosses the canyon rim; adults capture what they witnessed afterward.

**Pathway Expeditions (new).** Two adults take six students on a 6–7 day trip (Mammoth, the Sierra): 10+ hours of Socratic seminar on the drives — ambiently transcribed by voice AI so the teacher never breaks presence — AI-personalized projects built each night, and snowboarding, fly fishing, or mountain biking by day. Tutoring-grade ratios (Bloom's two-sigma effect) fused with life skills school never teaches. A semester course, deep relationships, and the wall between fun and academics demolished — in one week.

## The Curriculum Claim

Enduring wisdom, hyperaccelerated: Scripture and the canon that survived twenty centuries (Plato to Dostoevsky, Euclid to Einstein) as the foundation — plus frontier AI tools applied to each student's emerging superpower in supervised, creation-only studio blocks. *Screens for creation, in community, on purpose; never for consumption.*

## Built by Pacifica — Solid Core, Open Edges

Hypothesis: no ed-tech on the market that a guided Pacifica student or teacher plus AI can't replace with something better-fit for our school. **The Forge** — a school-hosted, student-friendly pipeline (laptop → dev server → reviewed production) — turns student ideas into shipped school features in weeks while teaching the real AI infrastructure stack. The school manages the Pathway core; a permissioned API lets student- and teacher-built apps extend its edges. Open-source LLMs run on an on-campus cluster so formation data never leaves the building — with solar near-term and a Radiant-class microreactor as the stated energy moonshot.

## Guardrails

Humans author, AI drafts — nothing reaches a family unsigned by a named adult. Recording only with written consent, in instructional settings, raw audio deleted on schedule; Lost Canyon and pastoral conversations are never recorded. FERPA-grade privacy; student data never trains outside models; faculty and a theological review group own the formation language.

## Proving It

Pre-registered metrics: graduate pathway diversity and fit, senior proof-of-work portfolios, expedition mastery vs. classroom equivalents, and 1/5/10-year alumni outcomes (purpose, faith, contribution) benchmarked nationally. Within five years: *our graduates know why they're here, and it shows in what they do for others.*

## Timeline & The Ask

**Fall 2026 (now):** Advanced AI Agents class shipping student software laptop → dev → production; capture pilot with 8–10 teachers; Lost Canyon run as first structured harvest; expedition proof-of-concept. **Winter 2026–27:** the admissions survey generates a primary Formation Pathway for every candidate, and the AI Lab goes live (~$26K one-time: 4 Mac mini agent nodes, dev + production servers, an on-campus LLM server, 4 student dev stations). **Spring 2027:** pilots complete with published results; platform foundation v1 built (data model, consent, portraits, Engine, Edges API); Fall 2027 full-school rollout plan to the board. **The ask:** endorse the principle, fund the AI Lab and pilots, and charter a privacy-and-theology working group.

## The Endgame

Thousands of Christian schools have the people but not the platform. Perfect Formation Pathways at Pacifica, publish the results, then license the operating system of Christian formation education to mission-aligned schools — Pacifica as flagship and standard-bearer. Being the top Christian high school in the world is the milestone; equipping a thousand schools like ours is the mission.
